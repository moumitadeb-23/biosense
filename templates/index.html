END-TO-END WORKFLOW

From Raw Culture Data to Automated Research Reports

BioSense bridges wet-lab microbiology experiments with data science and machine learning through a structured four-stage analytical pipeline.

1
Ingestion & Validation

Upload CSV growth time-series or AST records. The ingestion engine screens for schema integrity, biological constraints (pH 0–14, positive OD), and formula injection attacks.

2
Growth Kinetics

Fit empirical time vs OD curves, isolate the exponential growth interval, extract the maximum specific growth rate (μmax), compute doubling times (td), and simulate logistic regimes.

3
Susceptibility Profiling

Map disk diffusion zone diameters or MIC assays against established EUCAST and CLSI clinical breakpoints, producing strict dataset-derived resistance categorizations (S/I/R).

4
ML Lab & PDF Reports

Train Scikit-Learn classification & regression models with leak-free preprocessing, compute permutation feature importances, detect outliers, and export publication-ready 13-section PDF reports.
