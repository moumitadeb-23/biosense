Heuristic Phase Interpretation
EDUCATIONAL INTERPRETATION

Bacterial growth in closed batch culture undergoes four classical thermodynamic regimes:

1. Lag Phase (Adaptation) 0h – 2.0h
Physiological adaptation, enzyme synthesis, and repair; no net population expansion.
2. Log / Exponential Phase (μmax) 2.0h – 10.0h
Maximal binary fission division where doubling rate is constant: dN/dt = μN.
3. Stationary Phase (Equilibrium K) 10.0h – 24.0h
Nutrient exhaustion and metabolic waste accumulation; division rate equals death rate.
4. Decline / Death Phase > 24.0h
Exponential cell lysis and loss of culture viability under prolonged starvation.
Governing Mathematical Equations
Verhulst Sigmoidal Logistic Model:
N(t) = K / [ 1 + ((K - N₀)/N₀) · e-rt ]
Exponential Growth Rate (μ):
μ = [ ln(N₂) - ln(N₁) ] / (t₂ - t₁)
Doubling Time (Generation Time):
td = ln(2) / μmax ≈ 0.69315 / μmax
Underlying Assumptions: Assumes a closed, well-mixed batch culture with homogenous nutrient distribution, constant incubation temperature, and consistent pH buffering.
